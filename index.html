🔒 Privacy, Safety & Your Data

Plain-English answers for parents and mentors. Last updated: November 2026.

What we store

  • ✓
    Only what you type in: each student's first name (or nickname), age, avatar, stage, milestone ticks and your notes.
  • ✓
    It is saved only on this device (inside the app, or in your browser's storage on the website). It is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • ✓
    No accounts, no email addresses, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • ✓
    Tip: use a nickname or first name only. You don't need to enter a full name, school or photo.

Keeping your data safe

  • 1
    Download a backup from the Dashboard (⬇️ Backup) every week or so. We'll remind you. Keep the file somewhere safe — it contains your child's progress.
  • 2
    On iPhone/iPad: Safari can delete website data that hasn't been used for 7 days. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen and open CodeKids from there — installed apps are protected from this.
  • 3
    Clearing your browser history/site data, or using a private window, will erase progress. Restore it with ⬆️ Restore and your backup file.
  • 4
    Progress does not sync between devices. To move to a new device, download a backup on the old one and restore it on the new one.
