# 👋 Welcome to CodeKids Academy: a 1-page guide for parents

**You don't need to know how to code.** Your job is to set up the activity, ask good questions, and celebrate. The app tells you what to do next.

## Getting started (5 minutes)
1. **Open CodeKids:** https://hinga92.github.io/CodeKids-academy/
   - On iPhone/iPad, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**. On Android, tap **⋮ → Add to Home screen** (or install the app if you were sent it).
2. **Dashboard → ➕ Add Student.** A first name or nickname is enough. Enter the age; CodeKids suggests a starting stage.
3. **Tap your child's card** and look at **🧭 Next up**. It shows the next milestone and a matching project.
4. **Open the project.** It has steps, "done when" checks and a stretch challenge. Tap 🖨️ to print it.

## Each week (20–45 minutes)
- 📅 Pick a regular time, e.g. Sunday afternoon. Turn on the **weekly reminder** (Privacy page) in the app.
- 🎯 Do **one** project or milestone. Short and fun beats long and tiring.
- ✅ When your child meets a "done when" check, **tick the milestone**. 🎉
- 📎 Optional: tap **Add proof** to save a link or a note about what they made. It builds a portfolio.
- 📋 Sunday evening: **Dashboard → Weekly check-in → Share**, and send it to the organiser.

## The 4 magic questions (when your child is stuck)
1. "What did you **expect** to happen?"
2. "What **actually** happened?"
3. "Which **step** do you think caused it?"
4. "What could you **try** next?"

Mistakes are called **bugs**, and fixing them (**debugging**) is the most important skill in coding. Let your child struggle for a minute before you help.

## Levels inside each stage
- 🧭 **Explorer:** try it with help
- 🔧 **Builder:** do it on their own
- 🚀 **Creator:** make something original, test it with someone, explain it

## Safety
- 🔒 Everything stays on your device: no account, no ads, no tracking.
- 🛡️ Before opening outside websites, CodeKids asks a quick grown-up maths question.
- 👪 Some sites (GitHub, Discord, AI tools) need an account and have minimum ages. **You** create and own those accounts.
- 💾 Tap **Backup** now and then to save a copy of your progress.

**Questions or problems?** Message the organiser. Every bit of feedback helps! 💜
