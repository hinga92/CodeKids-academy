# End-of-pilot interview (15 minutes)

Listen more than you talk. Ask about **what they did**, not what they think they would do. Note quotes word for word.

## Warm-up (2 min)
1. How did the 4 weeks go overall?
2. Which child or children took part, and how old are they?

## What actually happened (5 min)
3. Walk me through the **last time** you used CodeKids. What did you do, step by step?
4. Which week was hardest to fit in, and why?
5. What's the **one project** your child enjoyed most? Why?
6. Was there a moment when you or your child got stuck? What did you do?

## The app (5 min)
7. What was **confusing** in the app? (Ask them to show you on their phone if possible.)
8. Did you use **Next up**, the **project briefs**, **Add proof**, the **report**, or the **reminder**? Which helped most? Which did you ignore?
9. Did the **standards codes** (e.g. 1A-AP-10) mean anything to you? Useful or noise?
10. Did the grown-up check before links feel OK, or annoying?

## Value (3 min)
11. On a scale of 0–10, how likely are you to recommend CodeKids to another parent? Why that number?
12. If CodeKids disappeared tomorrow, how would you feel? (Very disappointed / Somewhat / Not really)
13. Would you pay for anything extra? (e.g. printed kits, live sessions, a club version). Ask for **no price**, just what and why.
14. Anything else we should know?

## After the call
Write down in `pilot_tracker.csv` (summary columns):
- NPS (0–10)
- "Very disappointed?" (Y/N)
- Top blocker
- Top delight
- One quote
